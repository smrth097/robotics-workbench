Core Concepts

    

      
01 • SO(3)
Rotation Matrices
Columns hold rotated X, Y, Z axes; p_A = R · p_B.
RᵀR = I , det(R) = +1

      
02 • SE(3)
Euler, Quaternions & Gimbal Lock
ZYX pitch at ±90° hits gimbal lock; quaternions and SE(3) avoid it.
T = [ R  p ; 0  1 ]

      
03 • FK / IK / DH
Kinematics & DH Chains
Chain link transforms T_{i-1}^i along joint coordinates.
T_0^n = T_0^1 · T_1^2 · ... · T_{n-1}^n

      
04 • Dynamics & ML
Jacobian, RK4 & Neural IK
Simulate M(θ)θ̈ + C(θ,θ̇)θ̇ + G(θ) + Bθ̇ = τ and predict IK via MLP.
τ = M(θ)v + C(θ,θ̇)θ̇ + G(θ)

Beginners Guide & Demos

Complete scratch-to-advanced walkthrough, interactive demos, exports, console debugging, and a 45-minute lab activity.

1. Beginner: Scratch Setup, Camera & Joint Jogging

  • Serve over HTTP: Run python3 -m http.server 8080 and open http://localhost:8080 so ES modules load properly.
  • Viewport navigation: Left-drag empty space to orbit, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan, and scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Joint jogging: Drag robot links directly in 3D or adjust joint sliders within mechanical limits.
  • Auto performance mode: Runs in Auto mode with low-resource idle throttling and capped active DPR.

2. Intermediate: Kinematics, Trajectories & CoM Stability

  • Presets & reach: Compare robot presets (ABB-style 6-axis, UR5e, or lab rigs) to inspect joint frames, reach, and posture.
  • Quintic waypoint sampling: Generate smooth joint trajectories with continuous position, velocity, and acceleration profiles.
  • CoM projection: Check Center-of-Mass support projection to evaluate static balance across poses.

3. Advanced: Exports, URL Hash, Bridges & Console Tools

  • Exports & bridge: Export URDF XML and Denavit-Hartenberg (DH) JSON, copy shareable URL hash poses, and hook serial/WebSocket bridges.
  • DevTools & window.__wbTools: Open DevTools (F12) -> Console to inspect window.__wbTools, and hard-refresh (Ctrl + Shift + R with Disable cache checked) after updates.

4. Demos & Interactive Examples

  • 6-DOF Joint & Reach Demo: Select a 6-axis preset, jog joints via sliders or direct 3D dragging, and observe workspace reach.
  • Quintic Trajectory Demo: Sample smooth joint trajectories across waypoints and inspect motion profiles.
  • CoM & Export Demo: Check Center-of-Mass support projection and export URDF XML or DH parameter JSON.

1. Beginner Setup & Viewport Controls

Camera

Left-drag empty space to orbit, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.

Joint Jogging

Drag links directly in 3D or adjust joint sliders within mechanical limits.

Performance Modes

Pick Ultra, Balanced, or Lite from the Perf selector in the bottom-right corner.

2. Intermediate Kinematics, Trajectory & Stability

  • Switch presets (ABB-style 6-axis, UR5e, or lab rig) to compare link frames and reach.
  • Sample quintic waypoints for smooth joint trajectories and check CoM support projection.

3. Pro Exports, Bridge & Console Debugging

  • Export URDF XML or DH parameter JSON, copy shareable URL hash poses, or hook serial/WebSocket bridges.
  • Run node --check on js/*.js before pushing, and inspect window.__wbTools in DevTools Console (F12).
